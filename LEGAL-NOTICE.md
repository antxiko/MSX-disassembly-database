# Legal notice and attribution

*(También disponible [en castellano](AVISO-LEGAL.md).)*

## Who owns what

**The games are not ours.** This repository measures forty-seven disassemblies of
MSX games published between 1983 and 1989 by Konami, Hudson Soft, Activision, HAL
Laboratory, T&E Soft, Casio, ASCII, Gremlin Graphics, Melbourne House, Microids,
Loriciels, Topo Soft and others. All rights in each game remain with their
holders.

**No game is distributed here.** There is not a single ROM or tape image in this
repository: there are measurements *about* them — how many instructions a listing
has, which bytes two cartridges share, which catalogue number one carries hidden
inside. Reproducing the measurements requires placing the binaries where each
repository in the series expects them, and everyone supplies their own.

**What is ours** are this repository's tools, measurements, analysis and
documentation. Those are published under the licence in `LICENSE`.

## The quotations

The credit quotations shown on the site and in `datos/creditos.json` are
fragments of text the games themselves carry inside, reproduced in order to
**attribute authorship to those who hold it**. They are quoted verbatim, with
their position in the file, so that anyone can check them.

## What is owed to others

**Konami's hidden mark** — the catalogue number and katakana title tucked away at
the end of many of its cartridges — was discovered by **Manuel Pazos**
([@ManuelPazosMSX](https://github.com/gdx2)) in September 2021. This repository
merely sweeps for it across the series' binaries; the finding is his.

The **dump names** set aside under `metadatos_del_volcado` come from the
preservation archives' conventions (TOSEC, GoodMSX), the work of the community
that has spent years conserving this software.

## If you hold rights

If you hold rights over any of the games measured here and would like something
removed or corrected, open an issue in the repository and it will be addressed.
